# ProddyG Growth OS — reusable local growth system

Updated: 2026-09-25

## Purpose

This repository is a pilot implementation of the reusable ProddyG Growth OS. The system exists to turn verified business evidence into sustainable local-search, AI-search, visual-search and lead growth without mass-producing doorway pages or unsupported claims.

The operating loop is:

**Truth → Demand → Evidence → Asset → Distribution → Conversion → Measurement → Learning**

GrowthEngine creates and expands qualified demand capture. GrowthProof measures what happened, protects technical quality, and prevents unsupported claims from entering production.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. No location page is published only because a suburb keyword exists.
2. No claim ships without an evidence source in the client evidence/claims register.
3. No mass city/suburb substitution pages.
4. No backlink placement whose primary purpose is manipulating rankings.
5. No fabricated search volumes, ranking promises, review counts, project counts, delivery times or business facts.
6. Existing successful URLs are preserved unless there is a documented migration reason.
7. Search, local, AI, image/video and conversion performance are measured separately.
8. Human approval remains required for client outreach, review responses, paid placements, CRM/revenue reconciliation and other reputation-sensitive actions.

## GrowthEngine modules

### 1. Business Truth
Canonical business name, legal entity, phone, email, service area, GBP identity, social profiles, services, pricing/offer truth and approved claims.

### 2. Demand Radar
Use first-party Search Console queries, GBP query/category signals, site search behavior, sales questions, enquiry text and competitor gaps. Third-party keyword tools are supporting evidence, not ground truth.

### 3. Evidence Engine
Maintain a reusable evidence register containing verified jobs, service types, suburbs, dates, original images/video, client or agency, measurable property facts, testimonials and approved outcomes.

### 4. Local Coverage Engine
Default to a strong browseable service-area hub. A dedicated suburb/precinct page is eligible only when there is enough unique local evidence to make the page useful on its own.

Recommended ProddyG publication gate:
- multiple verified jobs in that place, OR
- one substantial local case study with original media and specific process/context,
- unique copy and media,
- useful local/service details,
- internal links from a real hierarchy,
- an honest conversion path.

If the proof gate is not met, keep the location in the service-area hub rather than creating a thin page.

### 5. Service & Problem Engine
Prioritize pages that match real buying questions: pricing, turnaround, preparation, process, deliverables, comparisons, specialist services, property types and campaign use cases. Word count is not a target; usefulness and evidence are.

### 6. Visual / Multimodal Engine
Original images and video are first-class search assets. Maintain descriptive alt text, relevant filenames, strong surrounding copy, preferred/OG images, VideoObject markup when appropriate, video sitemaps where useful and clean crawlable media URLs.

### 7. Authority Engine
Prefer legitimate earned authority: client/agency case studies, suppliers, associations, industry publications, partnerships, relevant directories and editorial contributions. Track every opportunity with owner, status, cost, evidence and outcome.

## GrowthProof modules

### 8. Technical Quality Gates
Every release should protect:
- canonical URLs
- sitemap inclusion
- robots/indexability
- structured-data validity
- internal-link integrity
- known spam/hacked URL handling
- analytics/event continuity
- performance budgets
- accessibility basics
- production HTTP smoke tests
- redirect and 404 behavior

### 9. Conversion Proof
Track form success, phone, email and WhatsApp clicks with consistent event names and lead IDs where technically appropriate. Separate traffic from enquiries, enquiries from booked jobs, and booked jobs from revenue.

### 10. Search & AI Measurement
Measure:
- Google Search queries/pages
- Google generative-AI performance where available
- web multimodal/image-led search where available
- image/video performance
- GBP visibility and actions
- GA4 acquisition and key events
- referral traffic from AI/search platforms when identifiable
- enquiry, booking and revenue proof

Do not treat a manually checked SERP position as proof of business impact.

## Opportunity lifecycle

Each opportunity should move through:
1. DETECTED
2. VALIDATED
3. EVIDENCE READY
4. PRODUCING
5. QA
6. PUBLISHED
7. INDEX/DISCOVERY
8. MEASURING
9. EXPAND / HOLD / RETIRE

Suggested scoring dimensions:
- commercial intent
- first-party impressions/demand
- evidence strength
- local relevance
- differentiation
- conversion fit
- competition
- production effort
- authority opportunity
- measurement readiness

## Reusable client onboarding contract

For every future ProddyG client create:
- entity-truth file
- claims-truth file
- evidence register
- services map
- local coverage map
- analytics/GBP/GSC connection inventory
- conversion-event contract
- opportunity backlog
- sitemap/index hygiene checks
- release QA gates
- monthly GrowthProof summary

The implementation can differ by stack (static, WordPress, Next.js, etc.); the truth/evidence/measurement contract should stay consistent.

## Sixth Vision pilot

Residential and Commercial are the proving ground. They should not be cloned blindly into other clients. What is reusable is the operating system: evidence gates, opportunity scoring, technical QA, local architecture, authority workflow and measurement loop.

During active Google ranking/spam rollouts, avoid unnecessary broad ranking-sensitive changes. Prefer measurement, evidence collection, technical hygiene and small standards-alignment updates until the rollout stabilizes.

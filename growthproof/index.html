Sixth Vision Residential GrowthProof

GrowthProof helps the authorised Sixth Vision team understand how people find sixthvision.com.au, which enquiries are recorded, and what changes have been made to the website.

Reports stay in the assigned Google Sheet. This page explains the reporting app. It does not display private reports or grant access to them. Ask your Sixth Vision administrator for your assigned report.

What the app does

  • Reads the connected website's Google Search Console and Google Analytics reports.
  • Writes daily baselines, query and landing-page evidence, event summaries, and operational logs to the assigned GrowthProof Sheet.
  • Combines those reports with lead and change records entered in that Sheet to produce weekly summaries.
  • Refreshes on a daily schedule while the authorised Google connection remains valid.

Your Google permissions

Google asks for read-only access to Search Console and Analytics, access to the spreadsheet where the app is installed, permission to connect to Google's APIs, and permission to run the scheduled refresh. The app does not change Search Console or Analytics settings.

The Residential and Commercial reports use separate Google authorisations and separate Sheets. Access to one does not grant access to the other.

Understand the data

Source reporting can be delayed or incomplete. An analytics event is not proof of a qualified enquiry, booked shoot, or revenue. Review source dates, connection status, and business records before drawing conclusions.
